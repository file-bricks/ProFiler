2. TARGET PERSONAS & USER JOURNEYS
================================================================================
1. Legal, Compliance & Privacy Officers (DSGVO / GDPR Compliance)
   - Needs: Rapid identification of personal identifiable information (PII), IBANs, tax IDs, and confidential credentials across extensive legacy file archives before sharing or archiving.
   - Pain Points: High regulatory risk from accidental leaks; SaaS tools that upload sensitive files to third-party cloud servers during scanning.
   - ProFiler Solution: Built-in Datenschutzampel (PII pattern detector) flagging sensitive files with green/yellow/red status indicators, in-memory redaction tools, and JSON Schema v1 redacted workspace exports with zero disk leakage.

2. Academic Researchers, Historians & Archival Curators
   - Needs: Cataloging, organizing, and full-text searching heterogeneous document collections (PDF, DOCX, TXT, RTF, scanned historical documents).
   - Pain Points: Slow or fragmented search tools; scanned PDFs without embedded text layers; duplicate files wasting storage.
   - ProFiler Solution: Local SQLite indexing engine with Tesseract OCR integration, SHA-256 fingerprint deduplication, and companion SQLiteViewer for custom database inspections.

3. Small Business & Freelance Operations
   - Needs: Centralized local organization for invoices, client agreements, contracts, and business spreadsheets without paying recurring SaaS subscription fees.
   - Pain Points: Complex enterprise ECM systems (e.g. DocuWare, Alfresco) requiring dedicated IT management; vendor lock-in.
   - ProFiler Solution: Turnkey desktop PySide6 app with dark/light theme support, instant folder crawling, batch PDF encryption/decryption, and optional ProSync companion launcher for multi-folder sync.

4. Power Users & Data Sovereignty Advocates
   - Needs: Complete control over personal file collections, offline usability, predictable unprivileged operation, and seamless handling of hybrid cloud drives.
   - Pain Points: Native Windows Explorer lacking advanced OCR and deduplication; uncontrolled hydration of OneDrive placeholders causing disk bloat.
   - ProFiler Solution: 100% local-first architecture (INV-LOCAL-01), Cloud-Placeholder awareness preventing unwanted downloads (INV-PLACEHOLDER-05), and unprivileged RunAsInvoker process isolation (INV-UNPRIV-06).

4. COMPARATIVE POSITIONING & ECOSYSTEM SYNERGY
================================================================================
- vs. Cloud Document SaaS (DocuWare, Dropbox Business, Adobe Acrobat Cloud):
  ProFiler eliminates recurring monthly SaaS fees and guarantees 100% Zero-Egress data sovereignty. Files and search indexes never leave the user's hard drive.
- vs. Native OS Explorers (Windows Explorer, macOS Finder):
  Adds native Tesseract OCR for image-only scans, SHA-256 content hashing for duplicate detection, batch PDF encryption/redaction, and PII privacy checking.
- vs. Enterprise ECM Systems (Alfresco, Nextcloud):
  Zero server deployment required. Lightweight desktop installation with PySide6, zero Docker/Kubernetes complexity, and sub-second startup times.
- vs. Sibling Tool KnowledgeDigest (`file-bricks/knowledgedigest`):
  ProFiler focuses on desktop file manipulation, PDF processing, OCR, deduplication, and privacy review; KnowledgeDigest specializes in BM25 ranking, text chunking, and LLM-assisted summaries.
